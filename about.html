About TreadX

Our name is our promise

“Tread” is the path one takes. “X” is infinity. TreadX is about moving forward along your path while meeting the challenges that come with it.

Why we exist

Healthy teams succeed

Workplace mental health is vital for productivity, yet millions of workdays are lost each year to burnout, work-related stress, depression and anxiety.

At TreadX we believe a happy, emotionally supported team benefits leaders, managers and the whole organisation. Our corporate mental health and wellbeing services are specialised and tailored, aimed at sustainability and at the managers and HR teams who shape everyday work.

We prioritise early intervention through therapy, counselling, coaching, workshops and courses, delivered by qualified professionals using data-driven methods.

Our method

Four ideas behind every program

01

Syncretic methodology

We bring together evidence-based techniques from psychology, psychotherapy, mindfulness, coaching and organisational productivity into one practical program.

02

Experienced team

Our trainers and coaches are seasoned professionals, including improv artists, with a passion for teaching and empowering individuals and teams.

03

Interactive learning

We believe in learning by doing. Workshops are hands-on and engaging, so participants can apply what they learn straight away.

04

Result-oriented approach

Baseline tracking and performance monitoring before and after our services ensure the results are tangible and measurable.

Founder's message
“We're reimagining mental health in the workplace—not as a checkbox, but as a core driver of performance, connection, and human dignity.”
Qasim OvaisFounder & CEO, TreadX Pakistan

I founded TreadX after years of working across corporate systems and creative spaces. The common thread? People struggling silently. Our mission is to break that silence, with culturally relevant, evidence-based tools that help individuals and organisations thrive.

From counselling and workshops to creative interventions like applied improvisation, everything we do is rooted in one belief: everyone deserves to feel safe, seen, and supported at work.
