About TreadX

Workplace mental health, done properly

TreadX is a workplace mental health and employee wellbeing organisation. Our professional training, organisational development and consulting support that single purpose.

Our story

Healthy teams succeed

Workplace mental health is vital for productivity, yet millions of workdays are lost each year to burnout, work-related stress, depression and anxiety.

At TreadX we believe a happy, emotionally supported team benefits leaders, managers and the whole organisation. Our corporate mental health and wellbeing services are specialised and tailored, aimed at sustainability and at the managers and HR teams who shape everyday work.

We prioritise early intervention through therapy, counselling, coaching, workshops and courses, delivered by qualified professionals using data-driven methods.

Our name is our promise

“Tread” is the path one takes. “X” is infinity. TreadX is about moving forward along your path while meeting the challenges that come with it.

Our mission

Break the silence

To break the silence around mental health at work, with culturally relevant, evidence-based tools that help individuals and organisations thrive.

Our vision

Safe, seen and supported

A working world where everyone feels safe, seen and supported at work.
