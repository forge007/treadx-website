HR & Management Support

Practical support for managers and HR on everyday workplace mental health.

Who it supports

Managers, HR teams and senior leaders.

What TreadX provides

Legal compliance guidance, plus leadership workshops such as emotional intelligence, presentations and negotiations.

Organisational value

Confident managers and more consistent practice.

06

ISO 45003 Advisory & Training

Guidance on the first global standard for managing psychosocial risk within an occupational health and safety system.

Who it supports

Organisations that already run, or are building, a health and safety system.

What TreadX provides

Implementation training and practical strategies for integrating ISO 45003:2021 into your existing system.

Organisational value

Better wellbeing, higher productivity, lower absenteeism and turnover, and stronger compliance.

Common questions

Before you enquire

Short answers to what organisations ask us first.

Who are your services for?

Employers, HR teams, managers and whole organisations. Programs are for employees and leaders; services support the organisation itself.

Can you tailor the work to our organisation?

Yes. We start with a needs analysis and shape the work around your people, your pressures and your goals.

How do you measure results?

We track baselines and performance before and after our services, so the change is tangible and measurable.

Where do you work?

We have delivered sessions and events in Lahore, Karachi, Islamabad and Rawalpindi. Tell us your location and we will confirm the format.

What is ISO 45003?

It is the first global standard for managing psychosocial risk at work. We offer advisory support and training on putting it into practice.
