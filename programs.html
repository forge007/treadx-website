ISO 45003:2021 TrainingOrganisations

Overview

Practical strategies for managing psychosocial risks, built on the first global standard for psychological health and safety at work. It supports ISO 45001 and focuses on identifying and controlling workplace factors that affect psychological health.

Who it is for

Organisations, HR, and health and safety teams.

What you will learn

  • What ISO 45003:2021 is and how it supports ISO 45001
  • Common hazards: heavy workloads, poor management, harassment
  • Integrating the standard into your occupational health and safety system
