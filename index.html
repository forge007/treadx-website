Who we are

Making workplace mental health a priority

TreadX helps organisations understand workplace challenges and build healthier, more supportive and resilient working environments. Our specialist focus is workplace mental health and employee wellbeing, supported by professional training and consulting.

Our mission is to break the silence around mental health at work with culturally relevant, evidence-based tools that help individuals and organisations thrive.

About TreadX
Our name is our promise

“Tread” is the path one takes. “X” is infinity. TreadX is about moving forward along your path while meeting the challenges that come with it.

Challenges we address

Understanding the challenges. Creating better workplaces.

In Pakistan, workplace mental health is a growing concern and progress is slow. Many people struggle quietly while organisations lose workdays to stress, burnout and anxiety.

Workplace stress & anxiety

Demanding environments and job insecurity wear teams down over time.

Employee burnout

Millions of workdays are lost each year to burnout, work-related stress, depression and anxiety.

Mental wellbeing & stigma

Cultural taboos keep employees from speaking up until a problem is serious.

Psychological health & safety

Heavy workloads, poor management and harassment are psychosocial hazards that put people at risk.

Culture & support systems

Few workplaces have access to qualified help or clear pathways to it.

Work-life balance

Without policies and support systems, work-life balance becomes personal luck.

Psychological health & safety

Creating psychologically healthier workplaces

ISO 45003:2021 is the first global standard for managing psychosocial risks at work. It helps organisations identify and control the workplace factors that affect employees' psychological health.

Heavy workloadsDemands that outpace time, skills and resources.
Poor managementUnclear expectations and weak support.
HarassmentBehaviour that undermines safety and trust.
