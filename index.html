The problem

Silence is the expensive part

In Pakistan, workplace mental health is a growing concern, and progress is slow. Many people struggle quietly while organisations lose workdays to burnout, stress and anxiety.

How we approach it
01

Social stigma

Cultural taboos keep employees from speaking up until a problem is serious.

02

Limited professional support

Few workplaces have access to qualified help or clear pathways to it.

03

High stress, low security

Demanding environments and job insecurity wear teams down over time.

04

Little room for balance

Without policies and support systems, work-life balance becomes personal luck.
