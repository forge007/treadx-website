Before you write

Common questions

Short answers to what organisations ask us first.

Who are your programs for?

Employees, managers and HR teams, and whole organisations. We run workshops for staff and for senior leaders, and a full program for building a mental health support system at work.

Can you tailor a program to our team?

Yes. We start with a needs analysis and shape the sessions around your people, your pressures and your goals.

How do you measure results?

We track baselines and performance before and after our services, so the change is tangible and measurable.

Where do you work?

We have delivered sessions and events in Lahore, Karachi, Islamabad and Rawalpindi. Tell us your location in the form and we will confirm the format and logistics.

What is ISO 45003?

It is the first global standard for managing psychosocial risk at work. We offer training on putting it into practice. Read more
