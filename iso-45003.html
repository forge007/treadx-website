The standard

What is ISO 45003?

ISO 45003:2021 is the first global standard providing guidelines for managing psychosocial risks within an occupational health and safety management system.

It supports the implementation of ISO 45001 and focuses on identifying and controlling the workplace factors that affect employees' psychological health. It helps businesses assess how likely exposure to psychosocial hazards is, and how severe the resulting harm could be.

Psychosocial hazards

What puts people at risk

Hazards of a psychosocial nature include aspects of work organisation, social factors at work, the work environment, equipment and hazardous tasks. Common examples:

01

Heavy workloads

Demands that outpace people's time, skills and resources.

02

Poor management practices

Unclear expectations, weak support and inconsistent leadership.

03

Workplace harassment

Behaviour that undermines safety, dignity and trust at work.

What you gain

A healthier workplace, on paper and in practice

The standard aims to improve outcomes for people and for the organisation.

  • Better employee well-being and mental health
  • Higher productivity and job satisfaction
  • A stronger, more supportive workplace culture
  • Lower absenteeism and staff turnover
  • Stronger compliance with health and safety regulations
